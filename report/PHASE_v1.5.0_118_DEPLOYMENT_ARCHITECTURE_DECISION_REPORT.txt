1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 118 establishes the authoritative Deployment Architecture Decision Record
(ADR) and runtime capability evaluation for OpenSearch Web.

Supported deployment targets are formally classified into two support tiers:
- Tier 1 (Primary & Fully Supported): `local-first` (desktop/developer workstation)
  and `self-hosted` (Linux VPS / persistent container).
- Tier 2 (Restricted / Advisory): `serverless` (stateless/ephemeral cloud functions)
  and `cluster` (multi-instance behind an ingress reverse proxy).

Explicit platform non-assumptions are codified and enforced:
- Serverless filesystem writes (/tmp) are ephemeral and DO NOT persist across
  invocations or container redeployments.
- Process-memory rate limits DO NOT apply globally across dynamically scaled
  serverless instances or multi-instance clusters.
- SQLite strictly serializes writes through file locks and requires dedicated
  single-writer coordination.

Non-negotiable invariants are strictly preserved:
- Zero paid domains required (operates on localhost, 0.0.0.0, or private IPs).
- Zero mandatory paid services (no Redis, no external SaaS databases).
- Zero mandatory secrets (boots and serves search queries with empty .env).
- 100% functional local path out-of-the-box.
