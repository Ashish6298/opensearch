1. SCOPE & OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Provide safe in-memory algebraic solving, calculus derivatives, and pure LaTeX formula previews:
1. Linear Equation Solver:
   - Evaluates `solve ax + b = c` and `solve ax - b = c`.
   - Generates step-by-step algebraic isolation derivations.
2. Calculus First Derivative Solver:
   - Power rule evaluations for polynomials (e.g. `derivative of x^3`, `diff 2x^4`).
   - Step-by-step coefficient multiplication and exponent decrementing.
3. Mathematical Typography:
   - Emits LaTeX strings formatted for browser and terminal display without unsafe `eval()`.
