1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 116 delivers an accessible, zero-credential Demo Experience that works
completely offline without paid APIs, third-party credentials, or mandatory
network connectivity.

Demo data is honestly labeled (isDemo: true, source: 'demo', and prominent demo
notices in responses). External provider calls are strictly suppressed during
demo mode and recorded as skipped in the Privacy Receipt. A curated set of
representative queries for everyday users and developers returns high-quality,
authentic results.

Accessibility testing confirmed compliance with WCAG AA visible keyboard focus
indicators, reduced motion support (@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)),
semantic ARIA live regions, skip links, and resilient error states that never
leave a blank page on provider failure.
