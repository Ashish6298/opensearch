1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 41 adds automated sitemap discovery, streaming XML/text parsing, and recursive
sitemap ingestion into the @opensearch/crawler package. When crawling domains or seeds,
the crawler automatically identifies sitemap declarations from parsed `robots.txt`
(`Sitemap:` directives) or probes canonical root endpoints (`/sitemap.xml`). Discovered
sitemaps are fetched, decompressed (if `.xml.gz`), parsed into structured entries
(`<loc>`, `<lastmod>`, `<changefreq>`, `<priority>`), filtered against robots policies,
and enqueued into the Frontier with priority weighting.

2. KEY DELIVERABLES & ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Sitemap Data Models & Contracts (packages/crawler/src/sitemap/sitemap-types.ts)
   - SitemapUrlEntry: Schema with loc, lastmod, changefreq, priority, and sourceSitemap.
   - SitemapIndexEntry: Schema with loc and lastmod for nested child sitemaps.
   - ParsedSitemap: Discriminated union ('urlset' | 'sitemapindex' | 'text').
   - SitemapFetchResult & IngestSitemapsSummary: Structured metrics & crawl outcomes.

B. Streaming XML & Gzip Sitemap Parser (packages/crawler/src/sitemap/sitemap-parser.ts)
   - Handles standard <urlset> and <sitemapindex> schemas with regex extraction.
   - Clean entity unescaping (&amp;, &lt;, &gt;, &quot;, &apos;), whitespace trimming.
   - URL normalization and validation against RFC specifications.
   - Supports plain text sitemaps (.txt with one URL per line).
   - Transparent gzip decompression via Node.js native `zlib.gunzipSync` for .xml.gz files.
   - Configurable limits (maxEntries default: 50,000, maxFileSizeBytes default: 50MB).

C. Sitemap Ingestion Service (packages/crawler/src/sitemap/sitemap-service.ts)
   - Domain auto-discovery combining robots.txt directives and fallback /sitemap.xml.
   - Recursive sitemap index handling with strict depth caps (default: 3 levels) to
     prevent infinite recursive index loops.
   - Per-domain max sitemap ingestion limits (default: 20 sitemaps/domain).
   - Robots.txt compliance integration: evaluated before URLs enter the frontier queue.
   - Deduplication and repository persistence in UrlRepository.

D. Crawl Orchestrator Integration (packages/crawler/src/orchestrator/crawl-orchestrator.ts)
   - Ingests sitemaps prior to seed frontier expansion during crawl runs.
   - Extends CrawlRunSummary with `sitemapsIngested` and `sitemapUrlsDiscovered` metrics.
