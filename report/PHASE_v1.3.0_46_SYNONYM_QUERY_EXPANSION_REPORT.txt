1. SCOPE & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enhance search recall and vocabulary matching by introducing a zero-dependency
bidirectional synonym graph and query expansion mechanism in @opensearch/ranking.

2. IMPLEMENTATION SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Synonym Graph Engine (@opensearch/ranking/src/query/synonym-engine.ts):
  * Zero-dependency bidirectional graph mapping tech, web, programming, and action concepts.
  * Curated seed dictionary covering JS/TypeScript/Go/Rust/Python, docs/guides, cli/terminals, api/endpoints, etc.
  * Bidirectional depth-1 expansion with weighted candidate scoring (original terms: 1.0, synonyms: 0.6).
- Query Processing Integration (@opensearch/ranking/src/query/query-parser.ts & query-types.ts):
  * Extended ParsedQuery with expandedTerms (originalTerms, synonymTerms, termWeights, allTerms).
  * Safe expansion that preserves exact quoted phrases and structured operators (site:, filetype:, intitle:).
- Candidate Retrieval Integration (@opensearch/ranking/src/retrieval/candidate-retriever.ts):
  * Posting lists lookup retrieves postings for both original terms and synonym expansions.
- BM25 Relevance Scoring Integration (@opensearch/ranking/src/scorer/bm25-scorer.ts & ranking-engine.ts):
  * Multiplied term BM25 score contributions by assigned term weights (1.0 for original, 0.6 for synonyms).
  * Ensures exact match documents consistently rank higher than pure synonym match documents.
