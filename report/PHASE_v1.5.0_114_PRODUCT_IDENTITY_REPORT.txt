1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 114 establishes the official public product identity for the engine as
"OpenSearch Web" to prevent consumer and enterprise confusion with the Amazon
OpenSearch Project (AWS / Linux Foundation Elasticsearch fork) and clarifies its
relationship with the OpenSearch 1.1 browser autodiscovery specification.

All legal disclaimers explicitly declare non-affiliation with Amazon Web
Services, Inc. and state that no legal trademark clearance or ownership is
claimed. Internal package names (@opensearch/*) and canonical engine constants
(PROJECT_NAME = 'OpenSearch') are intentionally preserved for backward
compatibility and monorepo cohesion.
