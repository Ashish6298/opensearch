1. SCOPE & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Provide instant, natural-language timezone and world clock calculations (e.g., `time in tokyo`,
`3pm est to ist`, `utc to local`) directly in-memory.

2. IMPLEMENTATION SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Timezone Evaluator (@opensearch/ranking/src/answers/timezone-evaluator.ts):
  * Zero-dependency world clock and IANA timezone conversion evaluator.
  * Curated database of 20 major world cities (Tokyo, London, NYC, SF, Paris, Berlin, New Delhi, Beijing, Sydney, Dubai, etc.) and global timezone abbreviations (UTC, EST, PST, CST, IST, JST, GMT, BST, CET, AEST, etc.).
  * Natural language query support:
    - Direct world clock: `time in tokyo`, `what time is it in london`, `current time paris`
    - Cross-timezone conversion: `3pm est to ist`, `14:30 pst to gmt`, `9am utc in jst` with day-shift detection (+1 day / -1 day)
    - Coordinated Universal Time: `current utc`, `utc time`
- Instant Answer Pipeline Integration (@opensearch/ranking/src/answers/instant-answer-engine.ts):
  * Integrated `TimezoneEvaluator` into `InstantAnswerEngine.evaluate` with `type: 'timezone'`.
- Presentation & Rendering:
  * Formatted output badge `[world-clock]` showing localized time, date, IANA identifier, and timezone abbreviation across Web and CLI.
