1. OBJECTIVE & EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The objective of Phase 29 is to prevent the crawler from consuming uncontrolled
resources across free-tier compute, memory, disk, network, and target servers.
This was accomplished through seven core resource control mechanisms:
1. Configurable crawl limits (page ceilings, max depth, per-request timeouts, body limits).
2. Concurrency controls (bounded worker pool preventing process/CPU saturation).
3. Politeness delay (domain-aware delay tracking and robots.txt crawl-delay enforcement).
4. Queue limits and queue overflow safeguards.
5. Per-run retry budgets preventing runaway retry storms on failing endpoints.
6. Atomic progress checkpointing to disk (<crawlerDataDir>/crawl-checkpoint.json).
7. Comprehensive crawl run statistics and telemetry.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ARCHITECTURAL & IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Configuration Extensions (@opensearch/shared):
   - `CRAWLER_MAX_CONCURRENCY` (default: 1, range: 1..10)
   - `CRAWLER_RETRY_BUDGET` (default: 50, range: 0..1000)
   - `CRAWLER_CHECKPOINT_INTERVAL_PAGES` (default: 10, range: 1..500)
   - Full sanitization and validation in `loadConfig()` and `sanitizeConfigForLogging()`.

B. Bounded Worker Pool & Concurrency:
   - Orchestrator worker loops execute concurrently via `Promise.all` across `maxConcurrency` workers.
   - Atomic page reservation prevents concurrent workers from exceeding `maxPages`.
   - Domain-level timestamps enforce politeness delays across all concurrent workers.

C. Retry Budget & Storm Suppression:
   - Configurable per-run `retryBudget` (default: 50).
   - Each failed HTTP fetch decrements `retriesRemaining` and records `retriesConsumed`.
   - When budget is 0, subsequent failures are logged as `retryBudgetExhaustedCount` and fail immediately without compounding network backoff loops.

D. Atomic Crawl Checkpointing:
   - Progress checkpoints are serialized every `checkpointIntervalPages` fetched pages and upon crawl completion.
   - Written to `<crawlerDataDir>/crawl-checkpoint.json.tmp` and atomically renamed to prevent corruption.
   - Captures schema version, runId, timestamps, active options, and real-time stats.

E. Extended Crawl Statistics:
   - Added `retriesConsumed`, `retryBudgetExhaustedCount`, `activeWorkers`, `checkpointsSaved` to `CrawlRunStats`.
